## The eleven areas (full sheet)

### 01 — Artificial intelligence for business
**Description**: MB Consulting brings AI inside the business: assistants that answer customers, automations that remove repetitive work, content produced with method and control. First the process is mapped, then the tool is chosen.
**What it includes**: Process audit and map of AI opportunities *(analysis)* · Conversational assistants on the site, WhatsApp and social *(chatbots · agents)* · Marketing, quoting and customer-care automations *(workflow)* · Assisted production of copy, images and video *(content AI)* · Advanced WhatsApp Business: catalogues, automatic replies, flows *(whatsapp)* · CRM and automated replies, from first contact to after-sales *(crm)* · Team training and usage guidelines *(governance)*
**How it works**: It starts with a process audit: where time is lost, where customers wait for an answer, where the data doesn't talk to itself. Then the right assistant or automation is designed, tested with the team and measured. AI goes where it's needed, not everywhere.
**What you get**: Process map and prioritised AI plan · A live assistant on the site, WhatsApp or social · CRM automations and usage guidelines for the team
**CTA**: See what AI can do for you

### 02 — Positioning and rebranding
**Description**: The work that comes before everything else: understanding who you are, for whom, against whom. Rebranding grows from positioning, when the identity no longer tells the story of the business you have become.
**What it includes**: Market, competitor and audience analysis *(research)* · Brand platform: promise, tone, values *(strategy)* · Full rebrand: identity, visual system, guidelines *(design)* · Internal and external launch plan *(roll-out)* · Outsourced marketing direction *(continuity)*
**How it works**: Interviews, analysis of the market and competitors, a reading of how customers talk about the business. From there a written brand platform, then the visual system and the plan to carry it inside and outside the company.
**What you get**: Positioning document and brand platform · New identity with a usage manual · Roll-out plan across all channels
**CTA**: Ask for a positioning analysis

### 03 — Startups: from name to launch
**Description**: For those starting from zero, MB Consulting handles it all: model, naming, identity, site, e-commerce, social, first content. A single point of contact holding the phases and suppliers together.
**What it includes**: Idea validation and positioning *(strategy)* · Naming, domain, visual identity *(brand)* · Site, e-commerce, social channels *(build)* · Launch plan and first 90 days *(go-to-market)* · Coordination of photographers, developers, lawyers, printers *(direction)*
**How it works**: For a startup MB Consulting acts as the single director: it validates the idea, chooses the name, designs the identity, builds the site and e-commerce, opens the channels and coordinates photographers, developers, lawyers and printers. One point of contact, one calendar.
**What you get**: Naming with domain and trademark checks · Identity, site and social channels ready for launch · A first-90-days plan
**CTA**: Talk about your startup

### 04 — Brand identity and registration
**Description**: A trademark is not a drawing: it is a strategic choice that must be designed, drawn and protected. MB Consulting handles all three stages, including registration with UIBM and EUIPO.
**What it includes**: Prior-art search and trademark strategy *(analysis)* · Logo design, variants, usage manual *(design)* · Applications: print, digital, signage, packaging *(system)* · National and European registration *(protection)* · Refresh of heritage marks *(restyling)*
**How it works**: First the prior-art search, so as not to design something that can't be used. Then the mark itself, the variants, the applications. Finally filing with UIBM and EUIPO, followed through to registration.
**What you get**: Logo in every format with a usage manual · Applications across print, digital, signage and packaging · A filed and registered trademark
**CTA**: Design your trademark

### 05 — Packaging
**Description**: Long practice in designing food packaging, with a command of regulations, materials and the print supply chain; plus packaging projects for high-end perfumery and the automotive world.
**What it includes**: Compliant food packaging and labels *(food)* · Cases and gift boxes for perfumery and cosmetics *(luxury)* · Materials, finishes and colour proofs with the printer *(production)* · Range systems and product lines *(range)* · Renders and prototypes before print *(3D)*
**How it works**: Long practice in food packaging — regulations, labelling, materials and the print supply chain — with projects for high-end perfumery and automotive. Every pack begins with a render, goes through colour proofs with the printer and reaches the shelf as planned.
**What you get**: Graphic and structural design of the pack · 3D render and prototype · Print-ready files and support on press
**CTA**: Design your packaging

### 06 — Dynamic websites
**Description**: Sites that aren't static shop windows: manageable content, sections that change with the seasons and campaigns, integrations with newsletters, CRM and AI. Bespoke WordPress themes, no page builders, fast and indexable.
**What it includes**: Architecture, UX and design system *(project)* · Custom theme, no page builder *(development)* · Dynamic content: blog, news, catalogues, events *(cms)* · Multilingual and per-market versions *(languages)* · Integration with CRM, Brevo, WhatsApp, AI *(connections)*
**How it works**: Analysis of content and goals, wireframes, design system, bespoke theme development, staging, approval, go-live. The client gets a site they can update themselves, with training and documentation.
**What you get**: A WordPress site with a proprietary theme · A simple panel to update the content · Training, documentation and maintenance
**CTA**: Let's talk about your site

### 07 — Custom e-commerce
**Description**: Online stores built around the product and the client's way of selling: configurators, price lists by customer type, multi-currency, Italian and international payments, integrated shipping and invoicing. WooCommerce customised in depth, or dedicated software when needed.
**What it includes**: Analysis of the catalogue and the sales process *(discovery)* · Bespoke WooCommerce templates and an optimised checkout *(development)* · B2B/B2C price lists, configurators, variants *(selling)* · Nexi, PayPal, Stripe, multi-currency, shipping *(operations)* · Instagram Shop, Google Merchant, product feeds *(channels)*
**How it works**: It starts from the catalogue and the real way of selling: who buys, on which price list, with which logistics. Then bespoke WooCommerce development, or dedicated software when the store has rules a plugin can't cover. Real test purchases before going live.
**What you get**: An online store with an optimised checkout · Bespoke price lists, configurators and selling rules · Integrations with payments, shipping, invoicing and social channels
**CTA**: Design your store

### 08 — Bespoke software
**Description**: When a plugin isn't enough, MB Consulting writes the application: order management, B2B portals, admin panels, integrations between systems. The code stays with the client.
**What it includes**: Process analysis and prototype *(discovery)* · B2B order portals with price lists and configurators *(React · Node)* · Admin panels and approval flows *(back office)* · Integrations with ERP systems, email, WhatsApp *(API)* · Hosting, deployment and maintenance *(management)*

**What you get**: A working, documented web application · An admin panel and reports · Source code and production environment
**CTA**: Describe your process

### 09 — SEO and visibility

**How it works**: Technical audit, fixes, keyword research and a content plan; local presence on Google Business Profile and territorial pages; then link building and monthly measurement.
**What you get**: An audit with priorities and fixes applied · A content plan and optimised pages · A monthly ranking report
**CTA**: Get found

### 10 — Video and photography
**Description**: Being seen. MB Consulting produces the material that makes the difference today: video and reels for social and site, product photography, still life and lifestyle, with in-house direction and post-production.

**How it works**: Concept and shot plan, production in studio or on location, post-production and adaptation to the channels: vertical formats for social, long versions for the site and presentations, product photography ready for e-commerce and catalogues.

**CTA**: Tell the story in images
